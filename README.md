## I build products.

I’m Reason, an entrepreneur who started out designing interfaces in Dhaka in 2012 and shipped my first apps in 2015. I still love the whole arc of building a product, from sketching the first screen to seeing what people actually do with it.

### All in on AI

I’m going all in on AI. It’s changed how I build and what I want to build. I spend a lot of evenings on my Mac turning ideas into prototypes, using them myself, and seeing which ones are worth shipping.
